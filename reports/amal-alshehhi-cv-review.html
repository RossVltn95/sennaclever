Priority Fixes

1

Make the target transition explicit

The CV has a credible base, but recruiters should not need to infer the role Amal wants next.

Analytical UAE National with 6+ years across strategic operations, risk coordination, research, and stakeholder management, now targeting Business Analyst, Strategy Analyst, Operations Analyst, or Market Research Analyst roles in Abu Dhabi and the wider UAE.
2

Strengthen the career break with real project evidence

The current section explains upskilling, but it should show applied work: dashboards, market maps, analysis, case studies, or consulting outputs.

Example direction: "Built a Power BI dashboard and Excel analysis model using sample business data to track operational KPIs, identify process bottlenecks, and present improvement recommendations."
3

Move skills below certifications and projects

For a transition CV, projects and certifications prove the new direction more strongly than a skills list. Let the evidence come before the toolkit.

4

Reframe ADQ around outcomes

ADQ is the strongest employer signal. Its bullets should read less administrative and more like risk, process, budget, stakeholder, and analysis support.

Example direction: "Coordinated procurement tracking across divisions and projects, supporting timely allocation of resources and approximately 80% utilization of the annual departmental budget."
