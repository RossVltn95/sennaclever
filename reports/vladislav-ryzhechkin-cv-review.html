Priority Fixes

1

Rewrite the summary for the target role

The current summary is credible, but it should tell recruiters exactly what Vladislav is aiming for.

Private equity investment professional based in Abu Dhabi with 5+ years across PE, sell-side M&A, and equity research, seeking Associate roles focused on MENA private equity and direct investments. Experienced across ~$700M of transactions, 100+ company reviews, financial modeling, valuation, due diligence, and investment committee materials, with practical use of AI tools to accelerate research and analysis workflows.
2

Make current-role bullets outcome-led

The Family Office section is the most important part of the CV because it is current, local, and closest to PE. It needs sharper proof of judgment and impact.

Example direction: "Led budgeting and financial management for a $12M revenue F&B portfolio company, improving visibility into unit economics, cash flow, and management reporting for owner-level decision-making."
3

Replace passive deal language

"Part of the deal team" undersells the candidate. Use the strongest true verb: led, built, managed, coordinated, advised, executed, evaluated, negotiated support.

Example direction: "Managed due diligence and data-room workstreams for the $600M+ sale of Cordiant to Severgroup, supporting SPA inputs and price negotiation materials."
4

Reduce repetition in Novus Capital

Three consecutive sale bullets read like a transaction list. Keep the strongest one or combine them into one line, then use the space for measurable execution detail.
